What we cover

  • Private limited company, OPC and LLP incorporation

Company Incorporation & Secretarial: Common Questions

How long does it take to register a private limited company?

Once documents are ready, incorporation through the MCA SPICe+ form usually takes one to two weeks, depending on name approval and how quickly the Registrar processes the application.

What annual filings does a private limited company have?

Every company files its financial statements in Form AOC-4 and its annual return in Form MGT-7 or MGT-7A with the Registrar of Companies, and each director completes DIR-3 KYC. Income tax and GST filings are separate.
